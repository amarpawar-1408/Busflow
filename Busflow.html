Occupancy Signal

Five colours. One glance.

Every bus reports its crowd level from onboard sensors and driver check-ins, shown the same way at the stop, in-app and on the map.

⚪ Empty

Under 20% capacity. Almost the whole bus free.

🟢 Not crowded

20–45% capacity. Comfortable, seats available.

🟡 Moderate

45–70% capacity. Most seats taken, a few left.

🟠 Crowded

70–90% capacity. Standing room only.

🔴 Overcrowded

Over 90% capacity. BUSFLOW auto-suggests the next bus.

Built for Indian commutes

More than a bus tracker

Designed around how buses actually run in Indian cities — patchy signal, women's safety, regional languages and all.

Live GPS tracking

Every bus's exact location, speed and route progress updated every 15 seconds — see it approach in real time.

5-level crowd colours

White, green, yellow, orange or red — onboard sensors estimate occupancy so you know before it arrives whether you'll be standing, squeezed, or comfortable.

Automatic "next bus" alert

If the approaching bus is red, we instantly show the next one's ETA and occupancy so you're never stuck deciding.

Works without data — SMS fallback

Low signal near your stop? Text your stop code to 56070 and get ETA + crowd colour back by SMS, no app needed.

Women's safety mode

Highlights buses with a marshal or pink-seat availability, plus a one-tap panic alert linked to helpline 1091.

Hindi & regional languages

Switch the entire app to Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu or Kannada — because transit info should work for everyone.

Support

Need a hand?

Technical issues, wrong GPS pins or safety concerns — reach us however's easiest.

GPS pins can lag by 10–15 seconds in dense traffic or tunnels. If it's off by more than 500m for over 2 minutes, report it from the bus card so we can flag the device.
Occupancy is estimated from onboard sensors and updated at each stop. On sensor-less older buses, drivers self-report — tap "Suggest a correction" on the bus card to help us improve it.
Text your stop code (printed on the bus stop sign) to 56070. You'll get the next 2 buses' ETA and crowd colour by SMS.
Use the panic button in Women's Safety Mode, or call 112 (national) / 1091 (women's helpline) directly — both are one tap from the Help menu.
